This Privacy Policy describes how **VRAM Lab** (`vramlab.com`, the "Site") handles information when you visit.

## Information We Collect

The Site is a static website. We do not require accounts, and we do not directly collect personal information from visitors.

The Site may use privacy-respecting aggregate analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand overall traffic. This does not use cookies and does not track individual visitors.
